Return Policy

Last updated: October 2026

7-Day Return Window

You have 7 days from the delivery date to initiate a return. Products must be unused, in original packaging, with all seals intact.

Eligible Returns

  • Damaged or defective products
  • Wrong product received
  • Product does not match description

Non-Returnable Items

  • Products with broken seals or used packaging
  • Items purchased during clearance sales
  • Products damaged due to customer mishandling

How to Return

Contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos of the product and your order number. We'll arrange a pickup and process your refund or exchange within 3–5 business days.
