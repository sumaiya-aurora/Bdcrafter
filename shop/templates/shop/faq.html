How do I place an order? +
Simply browse our products, add items to your cart, then click "Proceed to Checkout." You don't need an account — you can order as a guest with just your name, phone, and address.
Do I need to create an account? +
No! You can place an order as a guest without registering. However, creating an account lets you track your orders, save addresses, and use your wishlist.
What payment methods are available? +
Currently we accept Cash on Delivery (COD) — pay when you receive your order. Online payment via bKash, Nagad, Rocket, and Stripe is coming soon.
How long does delivery take? +
Dhaka deliveries typically take 1–2 business days. Outside Dhaka takes 2–5 business days depending on your location. You'll receive a call before delivery.
Can I cancel my order? +
Yes, you can cancel your order while it's in Pending or Confirmed status. Once it's been shipped, cancellation is no longer possible. Contact us immediately if you need to cancel.
What is your return policy? +
We offer a 7-day return policy for damaged or incorrect products. The product must be unused and in its original packaging. See our Return Policy for full details.
How do you ensure product quality? +
Product details and condition are shown on their individual pages. If an item arrives damaged or differs from its description, contact us for help.
