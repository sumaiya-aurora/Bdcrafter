Shipping Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Delivery Areas

We deliver across all 64 districts of Bangladesh. Metropolitan areas (Dhaka, Chittagong, Sylhet, Rajshahi, Khulna) get priority processing.

Delivery Time

Dhaka City
1–2 business days
Outside Dhaka
2–5 business days

Shipping Cost

Shipping is currently Free on all orders. We reserve the right to charge shipping on very large or heavy orders — this will always be shown before you confirm your order.

Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, we'll contact you via phone or WhatsApp with delivery details. You can also track your order status in your account dashboard.
